# The two paths: roadmap and discovery

The path is set by what research already exists (TRACES handbook scenarios). Decided.

- **Roadmap path (informed by synthesis).** Validated research exists on this exact problem. Frame tests and sharpens what the client arrives with.
- **Discovery path (fresh start).** No validated research exists on this exact problem, or only from other modules. Frame builds the picture from scratch.
- **Ad hoc** (a call with no prep) becomes one of the two once the follow-up call and the knowledge-base check are done.

## How the path maps to the TRACES handbook

The handbook (`traces-handbook.md` sections 6 to 9) names three scenarios: **Fresh start** (no research on this exact problem; research from a different module does not count; weight the workflow) is the **discovery path**. **Informed by synthesis** (research exists on this exact problem and can be read first; weight the request) is the **roadmap path**. **Ad hoc** (no warning, no prep) becomes one of the two once the follow-up is done. Decision flow: Q1 did I know this was coming? No means ad hoc. Q2 does research exist on this exact problem and can I read it first? Yes means informed, no (or other modules only) means fresh start.

## How the path is set

1. Step 2 gives the request a provisional label from its wording.
2. Step 4 confirms it by asking whether validated research exists on this exact problem.
3. Step 7 re-checks it.
4. Any switch is logged in the decisions log (date, stage `frame`, decision, artefact affected, who, alternative).
5. A solution idea in the request is logged as an assumption on either path.

Steps 1 to 7 are the same on both paths. After that:

| Step | Roadmap path | Discovery path |
|---|---|---|
| Research phase and conversion (8 to 10) | Light and targeted: fill only the step 7 gaps, or skip | Full loop: research, then convert to `.md` |
| Synthesis (11 to 13) | Short: does the evidence support or contradict what the client arrives with? | Full: insights, themes and personas |
| Problem space (14) | Built from research pack v1 plus any gap research, and may reframe the request | Scoped only after synthesis |
| AI opportunities (18) | Check the AI idea the client arrived with against the evidence | Left open until the problem is clear |
| Competitive scan (14a) | How others solved this specific solution | Wider, including adjacent categories |
| Brief emphasis | What we're assuming leads | Open questions lead |

## Flow notes

- Research pack v1 feeds Define problem space directly, skipping the research loop.
- Step 14 only scopes the problem space. The problem statement is written after the JTBDs in step 15 and becomes the brief's Problem section. Decided.
- HMW (how might we) happens in Explore, not in Frame. Frame's UX and AI opportunities are directions to test, drafted straight from the problem statement. Decided.
