## Ship step tracker

Part 1 Intake: S1. Part 2 Build: S2 to S4. Part 3 Handoff: S5. Part 4 Review: S6, S7. Part 5 Deliver: S8, S9.

Status values: not started, drafted, in check, passed, skipped (with reason). S3b is optional: run it only when the engineer asks for a React starter at checkpoint 1. Update at the end of every step. S2, S3 and S7 are checkpoints: S2 needs the design lead and the engineer to agree the stories before S3; S3 needs a person new to the flow to run the walkthrough (their words recorded) and the design lead to confirm before S4; S7 needs the named engineer's review recorded before S8. S2 may already hold early draft stories written while Explore and Harden ran; they stay `draft` until S2 is run.
